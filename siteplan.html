Site Name: Rabbits And You

It is a simple name that emphasizes the relationship between the user and their rabbit as a pet owner and pet.

Site Purpose

To provide an engaging way to learn about keeping rabbits as pets.

Scenarios

  • What kind of food should I get my rabbits?
  • What kind of rabbits are there?
  • What sort of enclosure should my rabbits live in?
  • Is there a website I can use for easy reference on important information?
  • How can I teach my kids about caring for a pet rabbit?
